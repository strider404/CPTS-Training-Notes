## Pre-Engagement

- **Definition:** The initial phase of a penetration test focused on preparation, discussion, and contractual agreements before any testing begins.

- **Primary Goal:** To define the *scope, objectives, and rules* of the test to ensure efficiency and alignment with client expectations.


## Foundational Legal and Administrative Steps

- **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA):**
	- A mandatory agreement signed by all parties to ensure confidentiality.
	  
	- **Types:**
	    - **Unilateral:** One party is obligated to confidentiality.
	      
	    - **Bilateral:** Both parties are obligated; this is the most common type.
	      
	    - **Multilateral:** More than two parties are obligated.

- **Authorization Verification:**
	- It is critical to confirm that the client contact has the legal authority to commission a penetration test.
	  
	- Authorized personnel typically include C-level executives (CEO, CTO, CISO), VPs, or senior management in IT, Audit, or Security.

- **Documentation:**
	- A series of formal documents must be prepared and signed. These include the NDA, Scope of Work (SoW), Rules of Engagement (RoE), and others.
	    
	- It is highly recommended that a lawyer reviews all documents.
