## Proof-of-Concept

- A **Proof-of-Concept (PoC)** is an exercise to demonstrate that a project or theory is feasible in principle.

- In information security, its primary purpose is to **prove that a security vulnerability exists** and is exploitable.

- A PoC can be simple **documentation** or a practical **script/code** that automates the exploitation.

- A significant risk exists when providing a PoC script: administrators or developers may focus narrowly on **preventing** that _specific script_ from working.
	- This approach is flawed because it **fails to address the underlying vulnerability,** leaving the system exposed to different methods of exploitation that target the same weakness.

- The goal of a security professional is to guide the client to fix the **root cause**, not just the symptom demonstrated by the PoC.
