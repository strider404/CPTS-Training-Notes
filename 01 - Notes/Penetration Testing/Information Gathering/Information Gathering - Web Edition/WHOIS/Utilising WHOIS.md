## Utilising WHOIS

- **Phishing Investigation**: WHOIS data can identify suspicious domains by revealing red flags such as:
	- Recent registration dates.
	  
	- Registrant information hidden by a privacy service.
	  
	- Use of name servers associated with "bulletproof" hosting providers.

- **Malware Analysis**: It helps in analyzing a threat actor's infrastructure by providing details on a *command-and-control (C2) server*'s domain, including:
	- Registration to an anonymous individual using a free email service.
	  
	- The registrant's location in a country known for cybercrime.
	  
	- Use of a registrar with historically lax abuse policies.


- **Threat Intelligence**: Analyzing WHOIS records from multiple campaigns can reveal a **threat actor**'s tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) through patterns like:
	- Domains registered in clusters before attacks.
	  
	- Consistent use of aliases or shared name servers, indicating a common infrastructure.
