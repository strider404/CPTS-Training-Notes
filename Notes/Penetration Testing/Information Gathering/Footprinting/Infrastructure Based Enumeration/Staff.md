## Staff

- **Objective:** To identify a **company's internal infrastructure**, technology stack, and potential security weaknesses by analyzing *publicly available information about its employees*. This process is a form of Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT).


- **Key Information Sources:**
	- **Job Postings:** These are highly valuable as they explicitly list required skills and technologies. This can *reveal programming languages* (e.g., Java, Python), *databases* (e.g., PostgreSQL, MySQL), *web frameworks* (e.g., Django, Flask), version control systems (e.g., Git), and other software (e.g., Atlassian Suite, Docker).
	  
	- **Professional Networks (e.g., LinkedIn, Xing):** Employee profiles contain information about their skills, career history, and current focus. This data helps confirm the technology stack and identify key personnel in development and security roles.
	  
	- **Public Repositories (e.g., GitHub):** Projects shared by employees can inadvertently expose sensitive information, such as *personal email addresses* or *hardcoded secrets* like JWT tokens.
